Finishes the terminology pass that [v0.2.0](changelog.html?slug=v0.2.0) started:
Conexões are now **[Vínculos](entry.html?slug=vinculos)** (4 Pessoais + 1
Organizacional, a fixed split instead of a free pool), and the standalone
Perícias list is retired — skill-style tests now just pick two
[Traços](entry.html?slug=tracos) per the fiction, with **[Experiências](entry.html?slug=experiencias)**
(short player-authored phrases, +2 and up) covering what training tiers used
to. `conexoes` and `pericias` are removed from the devlog since their
content lives under the new pages.

**New pages**

- [Experiências](entry.html?slug=experiencias) — the freeform background-phrase
  mechanic replacing trained skills: one bonus per test, and an automatic
  "does my character know this?" answer with no roll.
- [Random Ideas](entry.html?slug=random-ideas) — an open scratch page for
  loose ideas that don't have a home yet.

**Rules changes**

- [Testes de Perícias](entry.html?slug=testes-de-pericias) reworked around
  choosing Traços per scene instead of a fixed skill-to-test mapping, with
  new GM-facing guidance (and worked examples) on when a test is actually
  warranted versus automatic success/failure.
- [Condições](entry.html?slug=condicoes) and [Regras Sociais](entry.html?slug=regras-sociais)
  trimmed down — redundant examples cut, wording tightened. Condition
  removal (cure, rest, specific abilities) is flagged `#todo` for a general
  pass instead of being defined per-condition like before.
- Alcances' range diagrams were redrawn as SVGs.

Plus consistency edits for the Traços/Vínculos rename across `alcances`,
`comece-aqui`, `criacao-de-personagem`, `patrono` and `recursos`.
